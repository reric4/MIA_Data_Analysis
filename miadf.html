Merging Files into a single DB

merged = byalph.merge(bystate, how='outer', on=["Last_Name","First_Name","Conflict"])

This code merges the state and alphabetical dataframes based on the First_Name, Last_Name, and Confict, as these are the only columns that exist on both sheets, as well as being the standardized ones, and generally have no nulls. Since they are not in and of themselves unique, the combination of all three is needed to appropriately merge the dataframes.
